---
title: "mixed signals"
date: 2022-10-06
collections: [Poetry, Purple]
slug: mixed-signals
---
*you are not mine yet*
*but i am yours, i bet*
*from my endless giggles*
*to your warmth, those sniggles*
*you get me, but you don't*
*still you take my heart, i am blown*
*this rollercoaster is as calm as windy winey night*
*and as painful as a minute before the daylight*
*i am scared i will miss out on all of you*
*or maybe, we are just for those blue*
*this is driving me crazy*
*please, tell me if i am your daisy?*
*give me a sign, in looks or whistles*
*boy you're the king of mixed signals*

*how do you know that i needed to hear that*
*how did you just catch me in my breath*
*how do you take away my sorrow with your smells*
*but then how do you not know that you are all that this heart dwells*
*i need your help, i scream, i yell*
*am i yours or should the queen not keep hopes high? please tell?*
*you like when people refer us together*
*but you don't say no, no never*
*is this is a lost cause?*
*because I am counting on my flaws.*
*tell me before it gets too late.*
*atleast we should keep our stories straight*
*give me a sign, in looks or whistles*
*boy you're the king of mixed signals*

there are days when i just go absolutely mad and start cleaning and clearing everything. it's like my brain is not in control, and i'm probably trying to distract the chaos in my brain by cleaning the chaos that i can physically see. today was one of those days. but i couldn't finish the whole task of cleaning the chaos, unfortunately. because while i was moving the books around, a page fell out of my journal. the journal that i haven't opened in a really long time.

and that's really unlike me, because i carry that thing like it's my heart. it's like a beating version of me. i always carry it. if some thought just falls on me in the middle of a day, i do not want it to vanish in the air. that journal is the place where that thought gets written down. that journal has always carried the version of me which not many have seen. lately, however, i have let those moments vanish in the air. i've let them evaporate. because if i did write them, i probably wouldn't be able to survive what's going on.

the page that slipped out of the journal that day is one of the reasons i don't write in it anymore. it's a letter. dated february 14. i hate that day. why do we even celebrate that day? i've always hated valentine's. anyways.

i sat down on the floor and started reading that letter, something i should not have done. because it holds a version of me, and those black tunnels of memories that i really want to block off. it holds the joy and the happiness. and also the fear that i cannot admit to. that letter holds a warning on why you should not believe in magic. unfortunately, i don't learn from my mistakes.

i remember the exact day i wrote that letter. i was so exhausted. and something, i can't put my finger on what exactly, was weighing on me that day. i have always been known to be the person who leaves first. who runs away, who does not like to settle, to be known. i like the rush of moving into new rooms. that letter, however, did not indicate that. that letter indicated a very different version of me. and there went the floodgates. sometimes i wonder what exactly was contradictory about me when i was writing that particular letter. how can i barely know myself? and why was i acting like that?

for the first time in my life, i stayed. and i can write a whole book about that.

i'm terrible at surprises. i give away too much with a smile. i really cannot plan. but on that day, i did. and it came crashing and crumbling down. and i remember i just sat there, exhausted, but also sad and mad at the same time. i cried until three that night, because i felt that the version of this girl that i am, the girl who is writing this letter and planning surprises, should have seen this coming.

i'm the kind of person who always imagines the worst-case scenario in every chaotic situation which i can't control, so i can protect myself. when things go south, i build an immunity. it's like giving yourself poison enough times that you get immunised to the poison. i used to give myself small heartbreaks on a daily basis, so when it really hits, it will not matter much. that night was one of the ways for me to survive. i barely slept that night. and i think i was doing that on purpose. because i was trying to prevent many more nights that would keep me up, by staying up that night.

i was imagining how i'll run away this time. but then that girl wasn't the girl that i knew. and like an idiot, she pondered over it again and again and again. and soon she realised that she was projecting older wounds onto newer memories. she was bleeding on someone who didn't hurt her. but that's me protecting myself, right? i know how these kinds of things look. i'm just preventing myself from bigger harm. and i know it's unfair. but why do i need to be fair?

the why. that why still haunts me till today. because i had an exit strategy that night. i knew exactly how to leave. i knew exactly what excuse to make to him and to myself, and i knew exactly where to seek shelter after this. the door was open that night. if i had to, i could. but why did i not?

i stayed. i stayed because for the first time in my life, i felt this was something worth fighting for. i understood why it came crashing down. i liked his outline. i liked our outline. but i was trying to fit myself into an outline which wasn't truly me. and i knew that from the very beginning. but then i stayed. and it wasn't because of fear, because i knew this would end. this had to. this wasn't my story. this wasn't his story. but then i stayed. out of love? or maybe it was something i really, really didn't want to witness dying.

and now that i look back today, i feel i didn't stay because i couldn't see it dying. i stayed because i wanted to see him leave. i didn't want to carry the guilt of being the one who left first. i did not want to be the one who couldn't be vulnerable enough. and i'm okay with being left. my joy never depended on that. my happiness was never conditional on his existence in my life. and perhaps now i don't even fear being left, because i don't lose much. because you really can't lose somebody who you never really understood.

i stayed because i did want to understand. and i did not collapse. i felt abandoned, but for a really short time. i never felt lonely. i didn't hate myself this time. i didn't even hate that i did not leave first. because i wanted the ending. but i didn't want to call it.

i can live with not being chosen. i can live with the idea of being left. but i cannot bear the idea of giving up too early. of being the one who caused the story to be unfinished. i cannot be known as somebody who did not try. my biggest pride is that long after the ending, i have this quiet belief in myself that nothing can really break me, and i can survive anything. and i do not need to fill the void. there never really is a void inside me.

i don't know if it's good or pathetic, but this is how i love. i stay.

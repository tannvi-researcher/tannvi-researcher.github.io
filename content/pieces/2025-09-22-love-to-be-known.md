he is the only one who knew the stories that i probably didn't tell anyone before that. i've always known that i acted weird around him from the very beginning. even before it all started, even before the possibility of it had even struck me. i used to pick fights on things which were not necessary as such. things which were the most stupid. but that was an opportunity for me to talk a little bit more with him, i guess. and he kind of got it as well, and would mirror my behaviour. i don't know, it was flirting. it was weird, it was flirting.

the most important feeling in this world that i think we as humans crave is the feeling to be seen, to be noticed, to be understood, to be known. this is nothing like the butterflies. it's not short-lived. it doesn't hit you like a storm. this happens slowly, with time. when someone notices when you are jittery or excited, or look brighter or duller on certain days. i think you stop looking at the faults in that particular person, because you know them too well. and you mistake being so aware of somebody for probably being in love. it's like a cognitive dissonance. you're not able to differentiate. the fault lines in them start getting blurry.

and the knowledge you have about them, as to which nerve to pick on, when to start chatting, when to be there, when to give space, that knowledge becomes the weapon. it's not even about the circumstances of how you were, and where you were, in that particular time of your life. it's about the person who defrauds you into believing that they know more about you than you yourself, perhaps. it's the general clarity about how much time and distance it will take, and how much of a soft voice and loud statements i should make, to make this person believe what i'm saying. it's the hot and cold.

and now that i've passed this, i think i always knew the answer under all of that. i knew the answer of why it shaped up the way it did. and this left a kind of mark on me, which i don't think anybody can love out of me now.

and you know, when it's two in the night and you look back, and you think to yourself that i gave you the wound. i gave you the map to the wound, and the antidote. i told you where it'll hurt me, and told you how to help me with it. and you chose to do the very opposite. i told you what i hate the most about relationships. i told you what i hate the most about love. and you did exactly that. you waited until i hated that you knew me. you waited until i despised i ever liked you. as if i despised i ever gave you that key. i ever gave you that map. so yes, to be known is to be hurt.

and that's why i think that you should never date a friend. they know too much.

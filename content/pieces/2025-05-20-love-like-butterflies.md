i remember checking my phone a bazillion times for that one notification. i remember i'd found a way through which you could change the notification sound for a particular person's texts, and i did. because i really wanted to just pick up my phone and respond back when there was a certain kind of sound. it's like that scientific experiment they do on a mouse with a ring of a bell. the sense of nervousness, as well as hope for a reward in return. reward is an interesting use of word.

you don't know how it is supposed to feel like. you think this is how it's supposed to feel like. it feels so delicate and soft and naive and innocent. it feels like you're going to show the best version of you. the one who has the best grammar in place, and is funny and quirky. someone who's sarcastic but not rude, but also funny at the same time. you want to do things that they like, and you also want to show that you have passions of your own. you know, that kind of shows your personality.

i remember the first time i saw him. i was so nervous. his face was like a wind. maybe like a storm, a storm which has just hit me. and i'm just trying to get my hair away because of this wind in my face, so that i can just get a glimpse of him before he leaves. i remember i used to plan these elaborate things about how i'm going to plan the next day. wake up at 6 a.m. and go for walks, and the kind of lies i will tell my parents to get out of my house. i remember the time when just a brush of our forearms, while we are walking or sitting beside each other, was the biggest event of the day. i remember i used to rush back to the bus and sit on the last seat, looking outside, super nonchalant, so that when he comes, he doesn't think that i'm thinking about him at all. while all i have been doing is trying to figure out if my hair looks nice from a side angle.

those nerves are so beautiful and new in many ways. you never think of any of these things before someone comes. it's like puberty hits, and then you have all these weird ways of being attractive and interesting and being chosen. being chosen is an interesting use of word there. i think all you want to do at that point is to feel like you are worth it. to be chosen amongst the lot. and the lot is so small. it's like your classroom and your batch, and maybe a year junior, because, you know, that's fine, that's okay to do.

i don't think you ever feel like that ever again after the first time. those butterflies just go away. because that forearm touch would never feel the same after that. neither would that glimpse of someone. nor would you put in that much effort for anybody ever again. and that's why it's the most important thing to remember when you're looking back. i think this will be the first memory, and the first story i will tell my grandkids. because that is unmatched. i mean, whatever, good or bad, but it's unmatched.

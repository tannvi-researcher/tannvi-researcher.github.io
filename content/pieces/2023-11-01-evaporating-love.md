---
title: "evaporating love"
date: 2023-11-01
collections: [Blues, Poetry]

Wasn't it supposed to be easy?
I have grown up reading about it
Like running to him in the yellow farm
and then be rude but have that charm
Like dying but saying yes
and then dance with him & make him guess
Like being pretty, but clumsy
and talking until you miss your train
Like changing the meaning of life?

But I think I have grown up  to find that love is none of these things. Instead, the burn of heartbreak and letting go felt more steamy to be called LOVE.

Because my love did not vanish when he left. It did decrease when he was distant. It was cold on hot nights and hot on cold nights. It yearned for him to say it first, and killed itself when he didn't.

My love did not disappear, instead it EVAPORATES, slowly, steadily and fascinatingly. It goes away in drops and whosh... it is steam, and then air, no where to be seen, but I can feel it. It burns as it evaporates. It blazes on my skin, and smoulders my pride.

But some nights, the cold nights, it STAYS, in all its glory and beauty. It stays to help me withstand that cold night. It sticks on my skin, and makes me forget about the burn it caused.

But like every cold night, and like that LOVE, I too await for the warm sunny morning to come back, for it to burn again, and EVAPORATE.

—

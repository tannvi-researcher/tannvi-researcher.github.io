Inspired by Dodie Clark's "She", as if it was written by a woman for a man

(Verse 1)
have I lost,
or am I pretending that it is all alright?
is it okay to just keep believing that
atleast we made it out, alive?

(Chorus)
And he looks at me with those guessing eyes,
As if the smell of him, hasn't made me cover my lies.
He feels like home, far from home
But he also feels like a lot is left to be shown
He tastes like every birthday side,
But to him, I have nothing to hide

(Bridge)
So, I would never tell
I would never look directly at him
Or he will guess
That I have a lot to tell

(Verse 2)
Oh, he lingers his finger all over me
In the middle of us, chatting or sitting
Like my touch is setting him free
It is that I am right there, it is here, it is me

(Verse 3)
Oh, he smells like open grounds of green
Like all my lies are clean
And he touches me with a pain
Like he has been freed of that strain

(Chorus)
And he feels like home, far from home
And he feels like a romantic song
Oh, he is the memories of my polaroid pictures
And his fingernails scrub away my textures
And, he tastes like sleep
And he feels like a poetry too deep
And maybe he is everything to me
And maybe he really is everything to me

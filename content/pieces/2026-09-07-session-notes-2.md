how long does it take? how many years does it take to forget a moment? and how many years does it take to make a stranger family?

i remember this day so particularly. i was wearing my brown shorts and a grey t-shirt. i had just washed my hair. and he asked me how i'm taking the session, when he knew that my parents were in the next door room. i remember, i remember that i was chuckling too much, because i don't want my parents to think, why? i'm on a video call, we're just so quiet. i told him that i could tell them. i don't think they'll react in any bad way. but then their first question will be why. and i don't think i'll have the answer for that why.

and then he asked me, okay, you can't answer them on that why. can you answer yourself?

i remember saying, it's a service you give when someone cannot help themselves. it is like, i needed a nail to put a photograph in my room. and that's the hammer i get. and that's the hammer i can use to make my job easier.

and then he told me, very kindly, do you think it's going to work between us? and like always, i said something rational. i said something a 24-year-old should be saying, something i didn't want to say, but i had to. i said, it's okay. it's a service and we can end the contract.

i think this landed somewhere way deeper than it should have, at least for me. i mean, obviously, for me only. so we have terminated the contract. why does it feel like i'm terminating the relationship? this is way worse than actual goodbyes.

so i did the dirty maths under all of this. he didn't think we trusted each other. he didn't think i told him enough. he didn't think i understood why i'm doing it, the meaning behind it. he didn't think i was doing anything to improve myself. i was just describing it better. and he was right. he was so right. because i told him to push me and he didn't. i told him to give me space and he did. i gave him the booklet on tannvi, on how to deal with this client. and he didn't even open it. and that somehow is evidence of me not working enough towards this, quote unquote, service.

see, i tell stories about myself which i know will be liked. i am that version of myself which i know will be loved. and it usually works in most rooms. but for the first time, i decided not to do it. in this room, i decided to not decide anything. i only told him the truth about who i am. and then he didn't even scratch the surface. when i told him that i lied, he didn't do anything about it.

and to give this its true ending, i think the truth is that gym helps make bigger muscles, and bigger muscles store bigger pain. that's a joke.

so when i wrote [session notes #1](/2026/05/25/session-notes-1/), i thought i was being very clever. it turns out that self-diagnosis is not the way to go, and i was exactly doing that for many, many months. so the defeat is mine, it's nobody else's. it's when i talk constantly about becoming better and then do nothing about it. i just became better at explaining it.

and the solution is there, the solution is right there. the solution is that i can start again. and there's a version of me who wants to start again, who wants to see how i begin again, who wants to see what i changed this time around. but how can i get this year back? how can you just give your notebooks to someone else and ask them to give me this year back? that's not how you know someone. i can't do it from scratch. and the thought of doing it seems like i'm performing. and that makes me tired, if nothing else. and maybe that's a good word. tired.

so i'm not going to deal with this. i'm not going to solve this. i'm not even going to touch it. i'm going to just let this simmer on who i'm becoming. maybe this is character development. XD. or maybe someone will come and fix me. XD. or maybe i will just help myself when i have the time for it, and frankly, motivation for it. and why do i need to fix myself anyways? i'm good how i am.

and i was asked to leave. and this was the weirdest leavings of all. how can i leave a room i didn't even know? for the longest time, i think this was the first time it was a true version of me. it was the first time when i kept opening that door to check if someone's coming. i didn't wait for the footsteps this time.

and that's okay, i guess.

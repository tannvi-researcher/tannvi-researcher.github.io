piquant to my soul, corroding my scars
this is the new may, this is ours
whispers of  lame jokes and roasts
you charm me, I am overthrown

& I can see us lost in this gaze
slipped in those eyes of my craze
'Cause it is all mine
& I can see us twisted in bed sheets
jabbed my hands through your hair
'Cause it is all mine, all mine, now

You look at me from far
Head down, and eyes locked at the way I stroll
& I know that look, so I look away
Walk Straight, as I know this play
Smirking, trying to be serious
what is that you cause in me, I am furious

& I can see us lost in this gaze
slipped in those eyes of my craze
'Cause it is all mine
& I can see us twisted in bedsheets
jabbed my hands through your hair
'Cause it is all mine, all mine, now

Looking back, when I was waiting forever
One look was enough, for me one thought was enough
It was the hope that you'd come around
& pull me close and say, "I think I found"
The hope of it all, kept me alive
So, I pull out and recall
you weren't mine to lose
but I did lose, everytime you slipped off
will you ever be mine to lose, I thought?

& I can see us lost in this gaze
slipped in those eyes of my craze
'Cause it is all mine
& I can see us twisted in bedsheets
jabbed my hands through your hair
'Cause it is all mine, all mine, now

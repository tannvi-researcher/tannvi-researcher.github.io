title: "confessions"
date: 2025-01-31

i think we are all strange, sad, lonely, complicated and gullible. i think with this piece today, i want to tell you that we should look at those complicated parts of us. those parts which we did not filter out to make them look pretty. those parts which are sitting in some corners of your heart and your mind, which don't really seem to have words to them, and they defy interpretability and explainability. i want this to be an observation on things i do not wish to say out loud. actually, let's correct that. things i wish i could say aloud, but don't wish to as well, at the same time. this is like uncovering a shadow story. it's you, but it's not you. it's the ghost of you. it's the thing which doesn't really fit in this world. it's the part of you you don't want to see in the mirror. and maybe, in reading this, this resonates with you.

so i want to tell you that i am mad. i'm crazy. i listen to the same songs on repeat. sometimes, just to mess with the algorithm, i'll play a song all night because i want it to come up as number one in my spotify wrapped. i listen to that song until it's engraved in my body. and it makes me feel ill. it makes me want to barf. until i promise myself that i will never listen to it ever again. but let's be honest, i will. i will once i start forgetting how it starts. once i start forgetting where the drop hits.

and i want to tell you that i'm a sad person. sometimes i feel i'm pretending to be sad. sometimes i feel i don't know how to be sad. i think that sadness flows in my blood. i don't think anyone can love it out of me. but i think it helps. it helps me write poetry. pieces like this. things i can rarely say out loud, i put here. i smudge the ink with my sadness and the carelessness, which i think, which i think brings me sadness. i think i'm only understandable if you read between the lines.

i think happiness is a myth. i think happiness doesn't really exist in a vacuum. you can never truly be just happy. i can look at a sunset and still not be happy. i think what lingers behind happiness is the deadly silence of what's next, what's next, when's the shit hitting the floor. i think sometimes when i'm very happy, i want to cry. but i will not, because i know if i start, i might not stop. and then the world might just think that i'm mad and i'm sad. and i don't think they'll believe that my sadness is real.

i want to tell you that of all the people i've said i love you to, it's barely two or three. i think i only ever meant it once. how do we even know what love is when you're 17, or you're 20, or when you're 21? i didn't lie to them. i just did not know what love is supposed to be. and now that i know, i think i know. maybe i did lie to them. but the 30-year-old me would think that the 23-year-old me is lying. so i don't know what love is, but as of today, i think that i have only met love once. i think more than love, what i crave now is just attention. the want to be wanted, as many will say.

when i'm writing pieces like these, i wonder what is wrong with me. but then nothing is wrong with me. i'm just saying out things that people don't usually do. and maybe that's why i don't need therapy, because i can say it out loud instead. some days, i don't care what you think about me, because i think i'm great. and then the other days, i just want to be consumed by someone, so i can disappear. i think i'm doomed to be lonely. i think it brings out a side of me which happiness never can.

i want to tell you that i make a list of the things i want to do. sometimes i wonder, if i die before this list is finished, will i come back to finish it? and if i do come back, will i really finish it? because if i didn't live to finish it, why would i die to finish it? maybe if i come back, i will just sit in front of you and see how you smile, how your eyes sparkle, how someone else made you happier than i ever could. but i think i'd still be happy to see you happier. i think i'll be happy to just exist around you.

do you think people are ghosts because they're still trying to get acknowledgement of their existence? they're still trying to make themselves full. and so, instead of drifting between the worlds, they are just lingering around. they're lingering around because even in death, they are not able to let it go. they can't still call it even.

and i am the worst at letting go. i hold on to people, to memories, but not to places. i think it takes years to forget a moment, and a moment to forget years. and i know i will replay all of these conversations in my head a hundred more times. i will try to change the endings. hoping the next time is a better end, a better beginning. i will romanticise you, which is probably very unhealthy. but it makes things interesting, right? less mundane.

okay, i think i may be crazy. but for once, i think i'm okay with it.

Under my black knitted bra,
exists a girl beyond your power.

The girl who lost her fear,
and gave up on tears.

She doesn’t feel weak anymore.
Her vulnerability is now a symbol of her roar.

You can tie her hands,
and weaken her fight.
You can grasp her hair,
and bound her flight.
You can make her vulnerable,
and let her feel miserable.

But then, why can’t you be a man and let her be a woman.

Every time she says no,
why does man take that on his ego.

Every time she dreams
why is there no peace.

Every time she demands,
why can’t she have it in a charm.

Every time she is sold.
Every time she is asked to mould.
Every time she is crying
Every time she is asking for a life.

Why can’t we let her have a hold on her life?

darling start your count, with a one, two, three
because i have some spilling of tea
no, this was not about how it faints
but more about how i was blamed for those taints
i remember it from the very start
when i was pushed to be the target in the dart
from the dirt you pulled
to the stories you wooled
i was told that you weren't for the long run
guess i really suck at predicting the blow and gun
you pulled shit at me for not being upto mark
or maybe i should have levelled up to your arc?
(like seriously?)
but now i have grown over your hyprocrisy
and it's time to pull down the modesty

cut your tongue with the nemesis as we turn the wheel
babe, you are gonna repent every omission as i walk past on these heels

---
title: "Cloaked Echoes"
date: 2023-10-28
collections: [Poetry, Purple]
slug: cloaked-echoes
---
Do you recall that initial instance when you were labeled a bother?
How your breath seized within your chest, like fading twilight in your gaze,
Though your cheeks aflame, you knew not to interject.
Your voice, a melody lost within the labyrinth of your tongue, remained unspoken,
Your eyes fixed upon the ground, eleven years you carried that weight.

Now, a decade has passed, yet the glow in your eyes still wanes,
As you speak of your passions for too long, with constant apologies interspersed,
Your words, like fragile butterflies, fluttering over an endless precipice,
One you haven't leapt from in eleven long years.

But know this, my friend, you deserve to be heard,
For three minutes, for ten minutes, for two hours, forever.
Some may falter in the presence of your grace, beauty, wisdom, and heart,
Mostly because they struggle to hold their own.

Yet, remember, you were never, and will never be,
"excessive" or "overbearing."
"Though this tale embarked on a different journey, it invariably returns to you, doesn't it?"

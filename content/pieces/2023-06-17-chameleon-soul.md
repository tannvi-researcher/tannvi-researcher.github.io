(Verse 1)
In the depths of my being, I'm a chameleon soul,
An unusual girl with stories yet untold,
My mother's words echo, the truth they unfold,
No moral compass guiding, a path unconsoled.

(Pre-Chorus)
No fixed direction, no due north to follow,
I'm a wanderer, lost in the depths I wallow,
A kaleidoscope of colors, a spirit free,
An inner indecisiveness, wide as the sea.

(Chorus)
Chameleon soul, shifting shades in my core,
No boundaries can define me, forever I explore,
Unveiling the layers, embracing the unknown,
A symphony of contradictions, uniquely my own.

(Verse 2)
Through the winding tides of life's ebb and flow,
I resist the chains that try to box and control,
No fixed personality, no mold to fit,
Unafraid to be different, I refuse to quit.

(Bridge)
In the vast expanse of my wavering mind,
I find freedom in the uncertainties I find,
For in this ocean of choices, I've come to see,
That my true strength lies in embracing my identity.

(Chorus)
Chameleon soul, shifting shades in my core,
No boundaries can define me, forever I explore,
Unveiling the layers, embracing the unknown,
A symphony of contradictions, uniquely my own.

(Verse 3)
I dance with contradiction, I dwell in the gray,
Defying expectations, I chart my own way,
An enigma of existence, I break through the mold,
A chameleon soul, my story yet to be told.

(Outro)
No moral compass guides me, no fixed decree,
I'll ride the currents, my spirit running free,
For in this endless journey, I've come to find,
The beauty in being different, a soul redefined.

Chameleon soul, forever I'll embrace,
The depths of my being, the shadows and grace,
No limitations can bind me, for I'll never cease,
An unusual girl, I'll journey in eternal peace.

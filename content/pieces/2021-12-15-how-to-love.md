never have i ever felt this way before
and now my heart feels like a bore
no it isn't easy
but i have a found a way to make it look breezy
i know the heart will stop bleeding
but i hope it doesn't quit before it stops breathing
i hear my friends say how they break trust
and i wonder what went wrong between us
but i know i will remember you till the end of the time
you are in my songs and those rhymes
the bird of ours wasn't really dove
but i promise to remember how to love

i met you in the days of my glory
you said maybe it's time for our story
pulling my chair and giving me flowers
and now here i take lonely showers
i am ignoring how this heart beats
trying to forget how we covered ourselves in those sheets
it comes back to me, in tears and smiles
you really made it all worthwhile
while i tried so hard to hate you this time
but all i know is how to love with all the chime

staying till midnight, talking to you
i have so many memories that i am unable to unglue
i would be left with questions, who knew
hammering my head, like was it really you?

i still haven't changed my passwords
i laugh when i think of all the names i made for us
and the innumerable pictures from the high school
to the present, i kept like a jewel
who knew they'd haunt me, this cruel
and now i am stuck in this whirlpool
but i am lighting candles and firing homes
untying my hair and cleaning the combs
but thank you for being the one i knew
as now i know how to love and fall through

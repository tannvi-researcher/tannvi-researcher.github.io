title: "sweetest slow poison"

I woke up today
& then went back to sleep again
Because I didn't want us to be incomplete
Atleast not in my dream.

I woke up today
with you all over my body and mind
But this time I didn't sit and adore
Instead ran to the shower to clean.

I was asked by my roommate's boyfriend
What is love to you? How do you feel it?
And why is it always so different for you?
And I shivered and screamed in my head.

Like the first bite of the sweetest apple pie
& Like the smell of the pizza right out of the oven
Like wearing warm clothes after a hot shower when it is raining outside
and you just cleaned yourself up after a long day at work

Like driving with your hands tied behind your back
Like the first drop of the rollercoaster
Like a trip back home after months of pain
& Like that call from mom when I felt like everything was falling apart.

Like the first sip of tea in the morning
& Like the first gulp of beer after a long day at work
Like ecstacy with a twist of craziness
& Like the sweetest slow poison.

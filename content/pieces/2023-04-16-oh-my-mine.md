---
title: "oh my mine"
date: 2023-04-16
collections: [Poetry, Purple]
style: italic
slug: oh-my-mine
---
mutual ecstasy, inside and out.
rare capability? with no doubt.
this is a disaster for my heart.
how are we, we? I forgot.

a slice of calculations,
while I flirt, with those imitations!
a slice of instinct,
how are we stitched?
I don't want you to trust me,
she will run, I guarantee.

This is a love story, or my end?
help me, will you, my friend?
so the questions of right candidate?
am I too mechanical, in my ways?
did I miss out, is there a template?
or maybe I am too scared to trust, again?

you are not familiar with my love,
maybe that's why I put myself above.
Am I influenced by the ideal?
Or maybe you are right, gosh that would be unreal?
the ideal is more trust, & less of who you are.
the ideal is not warmth, but finding stars,
again and again, till you pour rains from those jet- black eyes,
and not cover yourself from those lies.

maybe happiness is too right, but not my right.
maybe it is balance, while someone holds me tight.
I need you to tease my compulsions,
so I change, scared of unending repercussions.

so, let us stop for now, while I sip that red wine.
yes, we will get through it all, we are fine.
there is nothing now, no line
I want my heart to confide
This is your sign
Oh my mine!

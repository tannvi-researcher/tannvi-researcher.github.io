i remember when you smiled
but were trying to hide it,
because you knew i would take offense.
i remember the way your eyes used to bend
when you were trying to do that coy smile.
specialty coffee had taken over you
like i never could.
the tangle in your wired earphones
is the way i wish i remained in your life.
but let's be honest,
i'm just living in the fragments
of who we used to be.

i left that house
because i could see a fingerprint in front of me
when you held me up.
it's imprinted in my memory even now.
i'm 300 kilometres away
and i still cannot leave that memory.
i remember lying on the ground.
didn't need a carpet,
because i was next to you.

this yellow light kills me alive.
i can't turn it on
without thinking of you twice.
we talked as if we had a whole life.
when you said you didn't want to lie,
and i said it was way too early to confess,
it burns in my memory.
but i didn't say yes.

my chest pains
when i think of you too, too much.
my hands shiver and go numb
if i don't think of you too much.
i think my skin holds
what my brain is trying to forget.
you have no idea how badly
i'm trying to shut it all down.

i went back to each one of those coffee shops
you took me to.
not because i was waiting for you to stand there
in your linen shirt
and your fake black birkenstocks.
i went because i wanted to remove you
from my last existing memory.
but that's the bittersweet
i swallowed that day.
even this sip reminds me of you.

sometimes i wake up imagining
that my house bell rang,
and you're standing there saying,
i asked your friends for the address
and i'm here.
and babe, i never left.

you know, i didn't cry
for the first four months.
i told myself i was healing
so much better than ever.
and then the flood started
with a single sudden flash.
i'm in a different dimension now.
it's gonna be two years since i met you,
and i can't seem to forget you.
cold brew tastes sweet now.
but i think i hate sweets now.

all i have to say now
is that i do not want to be a footnote in your story.
i want to be the girl who loved you
like no one ever could.
i want to be the girl you fumbled.
i want you to carry me till the end of it.
i want you to carry me like a scar
you can't seem to get over.
and while you're gone,
this is what i keep in return.
this burn,
this pulse.
this madness.

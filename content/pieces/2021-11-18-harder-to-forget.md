title: "harder to forget"
date: 2021-11-18
collections: [Blues, Poetry]

2 AM to 2 PM, I think of how you held me
And said I was all you'd ever need
I nodded and said sure
Hoping that this was just not to allure
But then I saw you leave
Guess I really mispercieve
But I promise you,
I'll be harder to forget, than to leave

I was walking through the subway
Looking at people holding hands in the day
I remember us,
How could you be that treacherous
Like a caleidoscope of memories pass my head
And just like that I die a new death

I fear you will be standing in that crowd
With another girl's magnetic cloud
You said, all's well that ends well
But what should I do about this hell
That I live in, under your spell

I don't know if you think about me
Or fear you'll see me in the glee
But I promise you,
I'll be harder to forget than to leave.

Friendship often ends in love, but love in friendship - never.

\~ Albert Camus

......

In the realm of shadows, we are the friends in grey,
Once lovers entwined, and now in disarray.
We are the Camusian question, and the Kafkaesque play,

Once intertwined, and now our hues don't blend.
Our love's echo whispers- can friendships mend?
In those  grey realms, where our paths are unbend.

And maybe you were right, we are the quiet of the fading day,
And Like that metal on the old bench of Cubbon park, you and I flake.
And here our friendship is in the grey, while these shadows gently sway.

Camus mused- that we were truly the absurdity in the air.
And now I see our love's fragments scattered, beyond repair.
And here I am screaming that- maybe we are friends rare!

And then I hear Kafka and that made it pinch.
Now, we are over, in a moment's flinch.
Should I let go or hold tight? Should I clinch?

Do we linger, as specters in the mist?
Unraveling threads that can't coexist.
But in this grey spectrum, our truths persist.

The truth that you were never mine.
And maybe our stars will never align.
So, what am I now trying to define?

A dance of memories, shadows cast,
Are we friends or the fragments, from the past?
In this grey palette, we are emotions amassed.

And here is Camus' absurdity- a silent chime,
And Kafkaesque twists- in the hands of time.
We are the friends in grey- atleast, a poetic rhyme.

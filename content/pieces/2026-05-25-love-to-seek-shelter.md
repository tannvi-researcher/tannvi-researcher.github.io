as a child, every book you read, every movie you watch, every song you listen to, describes that we as humans have come to find our other half. your goal. the person you go back to with all of your vulnerability, honesty and love. that one person you love without having any conditions in return. and that love without conditions is the love you're told you'll find in your partner. they tell you that that person will be the only place you'll probably be honest with. the only thing you'll probably be honest with.

i grew up as a hopeless romantic. i was willing to put my walls down, and find my shelter in someone else, to put my vulnerability down and give them my honesty. i was willing to feel the butterflies, to be known, and to feel the fire burn through me. i was waiting to be cracked open, to be read like a book.

and every time i reached somewhere close to that, it kept on asking for more. more for me to be okay with. more for me to swallow. to blur the line, to not realise who you've become in the process of loving somebody else. and somehow i was told that it's beautiful. we still write songs about these things. they call it the ache of being wanted, the suffering in order to obtain somebody. the loosening of self.

and if that's the honest explanation of how it's supposed to feel, if this is how love is, then i think we have just renamed and romanticised loneliness. we have dressed it up in a way that looks pretty and not desperate. how can one's willingness to ruin themselves be good for anyone? this cannot be noble. this cannot be why and how i'm supposed to be. this is not me.

we are building altars to things we know will light us up in fire. i think the hunger of being wanted is what has been called love. you needing to keep your house's doors cracked open, with the hope that you will see someone's face the next morning, is just loneliness which kills you on a daily basis. not love. you feeding yourself this expectation at 3 a.m. after you've put down something heavy from your life, there's no relief.

i think the cruelest part of all of this is that you cannot even rage about it. underneath all of these lies, things are unmoved. even if you are trying to be blunt and honest about the version of love that has been given to you like an heirloom, and told to you like mythology, you will still crack that door open. because you are waiting.

so perhaps love is loneliness.

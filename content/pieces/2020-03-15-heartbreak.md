When you’re deep in the mire of heartbreak, chances are that you feel pain somewhere in your body—probably in your chest or stomach. Some people describe it as a dull ache, others as piercing, while still others experience it as a crushing sensation.

But then sometimes, you have nothing to blame, sometimes you two are not hating each other. Not all heartbreaks have two people who hate each other at opposite ends. Sometimes people have torn apart because of resentment in spite of being madly in love with each other. At this point, your thoughts become contrary to your actions. You start questioning the very choices you’re fighting for. You beat yourself up and hug yourself tight for the same feelings.

This is that.

i've always been insecure about my nose. i don't know. it's too round, too small. not sharp. when i was young, i remember i used to put powder around it, because i saw some youtube video that said it makes it look thinner, sharper. i remember my dad used to say that it looks like a fritter. and i always hated that. i hated that i resembled certain people, and the patterns those people have engraved in them. there's always a trait associated with a certain kind of narrative, and my nose indicated that trait, it seems.

i remember my grandmom used to say, your nose has the rage of your mother and the indifference of your father. and i've come to realise that it's a lonely combination to have. indifferent rage? i am an extremely scared woman. i hate being vulnerable. i hate telling people that i'm vulnerable, that my nose is one of my vulnerabilities. and that probably branches out into my consistent and almost incessant need to be independent.

i think your 20s mark a period of self-discovery. you start seeing your fault lines, either by yourself or because people just point at them. i think one of my thickest fault lines, pointed out by almost every close relationship, is that i yearn. and for somebody who hates being dependent, i really want to be craved. i like the longing for me, the desire. but i also love craving someone and yearning for them. but the moment i see myself doing that, it feels like a crime. like i have tasted a forbidden fruit. and i'm scared that someone will see me with that glee on my face, that yearning to be wanted and to want something. and that, i think, comes out very desperately. and i think no one desires somebody who really wants affection. no one wants a desperate kind of love. but i think i need that need.

so all i wish to say is:

*in the blank canvas of my being, a pain lingers.
a lifetime of hide and seek screamed through these fingers.
i want to be craved, desired,
and yearned for, i plea.
but this is not how it is supposed to be.*

*it's the stolen joy, a coy bite.
a hunger, a stain.
that i hate.
i yearn the touch, the affair, the want.
but this is too much.
it consumes. it leaves.*

*there are marks on my body.
there are shadows i see.
i see their hands in my pocket.
there's a gaze i cannot unsee.*

*it's indifference packed in vulnerability and rage.
it's an affair of a refined woman with herself and her nose.
it's a whisper in the town about how she sold herself to the callings of the unknown.
it's a decree that no one wants desperation.
it's an echo that no one wants to see you want them.
it's a truth that hunger is ugly.
it's a january that's unforgiving.
it's an affair that's a crime.
and you are caught in a snare
and slipped on your face.
and you slipped on your face.*

*don't ask me why we cannot yearn again.
the yearning makes you stop believing in them.
the yearning keeps you alive to the end of the time.
the yearning throws salt on the wounds you wish healed faster.
the yearning will never let you love anybody else.
and perhaps the yearning makes you stop believing in love, after all.*

DRAFT 1

No, this ain’t about my first teenage love or crush

not even about how my cheeks were all blush

This is about the mistakes I made

The wrong cool, that I played

From the friends I made

To those shades of band-aid

Obsessing over people

While killing myself with needle

…

No this is not about how I was passionate

Not even about the side that was a romanticist

This is about the pressure I had from the way I compared

To be my best for people who never cared

Telling my mom that I have privacy

Trying so hard to just fit in this society

…

Never wanted to seem overdramatic to my friends

So, I let them touch me, and then ran home to cleanse

…

I grew up with those cheeky blush

Stood quiet as the world said hush hush

Where did I lose myself in this teenage rush?

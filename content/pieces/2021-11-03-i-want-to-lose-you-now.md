---
title: "i want to lose you now"
date: 2021-11-03
collections: [Poetry, Purple]
style: bold italic
slug: i-want-to-lose-you-now
---
...................................
i feel a little nauseous
when i think of all the signs of caution
i wonder how i missed on the lost eyes
when you broke those ties
...
i feel like you're close by
checking if i cry
it seems that i am being watched
but i am lost and scared
wondering if this is the game you played
...
i felt numb when i saw the real
and convinced myself that time would heal
but still i thought of you in the day and dawn
hoping that you'd erase this line drawn
...
but now i want you to beg right down
because i want to see you drown
i want to show you how it feels
when you see the trust's reveal
i want to see you cry
knowing that you'd never have it back
how hard you try
finally, having you face the loss and smack
i want, i want to lose you now
.................................

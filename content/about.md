**Hi, I’m Tannvi!** I’m a policy analyst at the [Centre for Responsible AI, IIT Madras](https://cerai.iitm.ac.in/), where I work on responsible AI, privacy and data governance, and on how new technology meets people and institutions. I started out in law and found my way into research and policy.

When I’m not doing that, I write poems and short stories, reread Camus, and keep a list of things to do before I die.

## How I got here

Here’s how I got to where I am…

I studied law at [CHRIST University](https://christuniversity.in/departments/main-campus/school-of-law/school-of-law) in Bengaluru from 2019 to 2024. Somewhere along the way I got pulled towards technology. I wrote and edited for [The Cyber Blog India](https://cyberblogindia.in/), did an advanced diploma in [cyber security and data protection law](https://nalsarpro.softpal.in/Study-Here/One-Year-Advanced-Diploma-Programme-on-offer/Cyber-Security-Data-Protection-Laws/About-the-Programme) at NALSAR, and took [Harvard’s CS50](https://harvardonline.harvard.edu/course/cs50-introduction-computer-science) because I wanted to understand the technology I kept talking about. In my third year I proposed a [Law and Technology Committee](https://ltcslcu.com/) at college, and in my fourth year I co-founded it, with a team of more than 25 students and a newsletter called Gizmo.

Law school was also, frankly, moots. I spent an unreasonable amount of it on the [Willem C. Vis Moot](https://www.vismoot.org/), which took me to Vienna in 2023, and on the [Jessup](https://www.ilsa.org/jessup/), where we reached the octo-finals of the international rounds in 2024. My dissertation was on how the law handles the personalisation of internet consumers’ data, and it’s [on SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7691703).

After graduating, I spent two years as an associate in the TMT and Digital+ team at [Cyril Amarchand Mangaldas](https://www.cyrilshroff.com/), advising technology and digital businesses on data protection, payments and new tech. The thing I’m proudest of from those years is small and unglamorous: I redesigned the firm’s DPDP gap-assessment questionnaire after watching clients struggle with the long legal one, and it brought turnaround down from about six months to one or two. Along the way I wrote op-eds for [Mint](https://www.livemint.com/opinion/online-views/ani-vs-openai-delhi-high-court-ruling-intellectual-property-ai-training-copyright-11785477510864.html), CNBC-TV18 and NDTV Profit.

In 2026 I decided it was time to do what I love, and I pivoted. Since August I’ve been at CeRAI, working on bystander privacy and on practical DPDP compliance for AI companies. On the side, I’m a researcher at the [Center for Democratic AI](https://centerfordemocraticai.org/), co-chair of media at the [Moot Alumni Association](https://maa.net/core-team/), and an arbitrator at the same Vis Moot I once competed in.

The full list lives on my [CV](/cv/), and my writing lives on [Scratch](/scratch/) and [Soapbox](/soapbox/).

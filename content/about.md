Hi, I’m Tannvi. My interests sit at the intersection of artificial intelligence, law, technology and public policy, particularly questions around responsible AI, privacy, data governance and how emerging technologies interact with people and institutions. I started out in law and have since found my way into research and policy. Along the way, I’ve worked, studied, competed, written, researched, coded a little, and changed my mind about what I want to do more than once.

## Now

Currently exploring responsible AI, AI governance and technology policy at [CeRAI, IIT Madras](https://cerai.iitm.ac.in/).

## Before this

- I practised technology, data protection and fintech law at [Cyril Amarchand Mangaldas](https://www.cyrilshroff.com/) for two years.
- Studied law at [Christ University, Bangalore](https://christuniversity.in/departments/main-campus/school-of-law/school-of-law), along with which I pursued Advanced Diploma in [Cyber Security & Data Protection Laws](https://nalsarpro.softpal.in/Study-Here/One-Year-Advanced-Diploma-Programme-on-offer/Cyber-Security-Data-Protection-Laws/About-the-Programme) at the NALSAR University of Law.
- Tried to understand the technology I kept talking about by taking [Harvard’s CS50: Introduction to Computer Science](https://harvardonline.harvard.edu/course/cs50-introduction-computer-science).
- Represented my university at the [Philip C. Jessup International Law Moot Court Competition](https://www.ilsa.org/jessup/), reaching the Octo-Finals of the 2024 International Rounds.
- Spent another unreasonable amount of law school doing the [Willem C. Vis Moot](https://www.vismoot.org/).

## Also

I also volunteer as *Researcher* at [Center for Democratic AI](https://centerfordemocraticai.org/), as *Co-chair of Media* at [Moot Alumni Association](https://maa.net/core-team/) and *Arbitrate* the [Willem C. Vis Moot](https://www.vismoot.org/).
